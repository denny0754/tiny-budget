# TinyBudget

TinyBudget is a headless, API-first personal finance and budgeting engine. Built for developers and power users, it strips away the overhead of managing a monolithic frontend to focus purely on the core logic of money management.

By abstracting budgeting into a robust API, TinyBudget allows you to interact with your financial data however you prefer—whether through custom scripts, third-party integrations, command-line tools, or a bespoke frontend you build later.

## Core Philosophy

- API-First: Complete separation of concerns. The backend handles the math, data integrity, and business logic; you decide how to interface with it.
- Agnostic: Bring your own UI, or don't use one at all.
- Self-Hostable: Designed to be lightweight and containerized, ready to deploy to your own infrastructure.
- Extensible: A clean foundation for tracking accounts, categorizing transactions, and enforcing budget limits without feature bloat.
